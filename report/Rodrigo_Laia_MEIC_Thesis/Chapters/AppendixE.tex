\chapter{Secondary No-Equal Ablation}
\label{chap:appendix-no-equal}

The decoder-controlled no-equal variant removes equal source-reference
rewriting examples from the supervised stages while retaining identification
examples. It uses the same Stage C continuation data as the matched
configuration. Stage C therefore differs through its supervised starting
checkpoint rather than through a separate continuation-data variant.

Removing equal rewriting examples produces only small changes on FRMT but
generally reduces Golden Collection rewriting performance, supporting retention
of equal examples in the supervised data. The reduction is consistent with the
copy-friendly structure of the Golden Collection discussed in
\Cref{sec:error-analysis-copy}, where many examples require little or no
rewriting.
