\noindent Portuguese is a pluricentric language, but large language models trained on generic web corpora are often shaped by the predominance of Brazilian Portuguese. This work studies European Portuguese (EP) and Brazilian Portuguese (BP) through two related tasks: sentence-level variety identification and bidirectional rewriting. Supervision is consolidated from OpenSubtitles, Few-shot Region-aware Machine Translation (FRMT), the Golden Collection, and a GPT-Wikipedia resource, with preprocessing and filtering designed to preserve variety-relevant differences while reducing unrelated paraphrase and alignment noise.

The experimental study adapts T5Gemma 2 encoder-decoder models with 270M and 4B parameters. The 270M-parameter model is fully fine-tuned for supervised protocol selection and separate reward-design diagnostics, while the 4B-parameter model is adapted with Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) to compare control placement, supervision composition, staged adaptation, and reward-based continuation. Supervision composition produces the largest rewriting changes. GPT-Wikipedia is most closely aligned with the conservative Golden Collection, while adding FRMT improves FRMT identification, changes FRMT rewriting depending on direction and control, and strongly lowers Golden Collection rewriting after supervised specialization. Decoder control is consistently stronger for BP-to-EP rewriting, whereas EP-to-BP remains mixed. Reward-based continuation changes GPT-Wikipedia conditions modestly but substantially recovers Golden Collection rewriting after GPT-Wikipedia + FRMT specialization. The results show that aligned EP/BP resources can induce different rewriting policies, while reference construction and metrics do not fully distinguish required adaptation, appropriate preservation, and broader reformulation.
