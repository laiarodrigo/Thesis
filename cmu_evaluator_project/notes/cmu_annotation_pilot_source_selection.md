# CMU annotation pilot: BP source selection

## Purpose and scope

This document selects 30 Brazilian Portuguese (BP) source items for the first annotation-guideline pilot: 10 from TED2020, 10 from FRMT, and 10 from DSL-TL. Candidate adaptations have **not** been generated. No error annotation, severity label, score, or human gold is included.

Selection used only the BP text. Corresponding European Portuguese (EP/PT-PT) sentences or references were not consulted to select items or infer a correct adaptation. The linguistic labels below are prospective coverage cues for later candidate evaluation, not claims that a particular edit is mandatory. In particular, “copy may be valid” is a source-only judgment that must be checked during the pilot.

The final items were chosen after screening larger preliminary pools from each resource for linguistic variety, plausible copy-valid cases, contextual usefulness, length, and source quality. The set is intentionally a mixed pilot rather than a collection of maximally obvious BP markers.

## Source inventory and context policy

- **TED2020:** BP-only raw transcripts from `data/external/TED2020/TED2020/raw/pt_br/`. The source-only archive is retained as `data/external/TED2020/pt_br.zip` (SHA-256: `40fa991a014fd7e875fd34ea7c195ff503b381a89fe9b78bb009f83c26bfa0fb`). Each ID below identifies the XML transcript and sentence ID; the immediately preceding and following BP sentences are preserved.
- **FRMT:** PT-BR-labelled source sentences from `data/encoder_decoder/t5gemma2/frmt_only/classification_test.jsonl`. The local representation is sentence-level, so document context is unavailable.
- **DSL-TL:** PT-BR-labelled rows from `data/external/DSL-TL/DSL-TL-Corpus/PT-DSL-TL/PT_train.tsv`. When a row contains multiple sentences, the complete row is preserved and the selected target is marked. No context outside the row is reconstructed.

### Balance and gaps

- **Likely copy-valid/shared cases:** 7 of 30 (TED 2, FRMT 3, DSL-TL 2). Three additional items are marked uncertain rather than being counted as copy-valid.
- **Items with usable surrounding context:** 17 of 30: all 10 TED targets plus 7 multi-sentence DSL-TL rows. FRMT contributes none because its local version is sentence-level.
- **Explicitly context-sensitive cases:** 5 of 30 (TED 2, DSL-TL 3).
- **Absent or not claimed:** no selected target provides sufficiently clear evidence for a null object or verb-phrase ellipsis. No target is labelled as a clear BP–EP multiword/phraseological contrast. These should remain documented gaps rather than being inferred from ambiguous examples.
- **Orthography:** the two selected cases use contemporary contrasts. Obsolete spellings found during screening were excluded rather than used to inflate coverage.

## Source-quality observations from screening

- **TED2020:** the BP-only OPUS archive contains 3,968 ordered transcript XML files and supplies recoverable sentence IDs. Screening found caption-boundary fragments, split quotations, occasional missing accents or typographical errors, and some awkward translated sentences. Items with obvious corruption were excluded; one authentic split quotation is retained for `s5.1` because its immediate context makes the boundary explicit.
- **FRMT:** the local sentence-level material includes fragments, name/number-heavy entries, awkward or evidently noisy translations, and highly specialized encyclopedic statements. The selected ten are complete PT-BR-labelled sentences; no PT-PT text was used, and no document context is asserted.
- **DSL-TL:** PT-BR labels are not by themselves a quality guarantee. Screening found malformed quotations, legacy spellings, sentence fragments, agreement errors, and some rows whose variety looked doubtful. Those rows were excluded. Complete multi-sentence extracts are retained only when the row itself supplies coherent context.
